The problem

The closest door isn't the fastest way to your seat.

Routing that thinks

Doors, hours, stairs versus elevator, PantherCard.

Pure Kotlin Dijkstra, 45 tests. Considers entrance × vertical method, card-only doors by time of day, avoid stairs, approved student shortcuts. The router takes "now" as a parameter.
